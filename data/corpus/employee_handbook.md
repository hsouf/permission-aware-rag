# Employee Handbook (Helix Labs)

## Time off
Everyone gets 25 days of paid time off per year plus public holidays. Book time off in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days.

## Remote work
Teams agree their own office days. The default expectation is two days a week in the office, with core collaboration hours from 11:00 to 15:00.

## Expenses
Submit receipts within 30 days. Travel must be booked through the travel portal. Meals while travelling are covered up to the daily allowance.

## How pay reviews work
Compensation is reviewed once a year in March. Salary bands are set per level using market data. Your manager can explain where you sit in your band. Individual salaries are confidential and are never shared in company channels.

## Asking questions
If you are not sure who owns a topic, ask in the #ask-people channel or email the People team.
